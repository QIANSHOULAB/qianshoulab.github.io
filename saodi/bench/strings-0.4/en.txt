fix_busy	Close whatever has it open (Excel, a photo viewer), then try again.

fix_gone	Probably just moved. Open the folder again.
err_exists	Name’s taken
fix_exists	Try again and I’ll pick another name.
err_toolong	Path too long
fix_toolong	Move it somewhere shallower and try again.
err_diskfull	Disk full
fix_diskfull	Free up some space and try again.
err_oserror	Windows said no
fix_oserror	It may be read-only or need admin rights.
err_password	Archive has a password
fix_password	That one’s yours to open.
err_badarchive	Archive is broken
fix_badarchive	Download a fresh copy and try again.

fix_toobig	Over 20 GB or 100,000 files inside. Not risking it.

fix_notarchive	The extension lied. Ignore it.

err_nothing	Nothing to undo
