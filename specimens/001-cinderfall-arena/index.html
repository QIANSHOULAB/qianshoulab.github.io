How math replaced the asset pipeline

1. Trigonometry takes over the art department

The old wall: language models are great at producing logic and hopeless at hand-modeling a Blender mesh with tens of thousands of vertices and skin weights.

The zero-flesh move:

  • Primitives as bones. Inside WebGL / Three.js, bodies, armor and weapons are assembled from boxes, spheres, cylinders and capsules.
  • Joints bent by formula. No animation files. On every requestAnimationFrame, Math.sin, Math.cos, spring-damper models and quaternion slerp compute the shoulder, elbow, waist and knee angles. The wind-up is stored angle, the swing is a burst of angular velocity, hit-stun is a jittering coordinate.
  • Spells painted by shaders. Sword trails, fireballs and healing circles use no sprite sheets. Custom fragment shaders and code-driven particle emitters rewrite the pixels on the spot.

2. Oscillators fake the voice

The old wall: digging through sound libraries, then trimming and aligning hundreds of swooshes and explosions, breaks a pure-code workflow in half.

The zero-flesh move:

  • Go straight to the browser’s Web Audio API and treat sound as what it really is: air pressure as a function of time.
  • Spin up oscillators (OscillatorNode: sine, saw, square) and white-noise buffers (AudioBuffer), then run them through BiquadFilterNode filters and ADSR GainNode envelopes.
  • The mapping: a blade swing is high-passed white noise with a steep exponential decay. A blunt hit is a sine wave dropping from 150 Hz to 30 Hz almost instantly. A spell chant is two oscillators in FM with reverb on top. Every sound is conjured by the sound card in the same millisecond the hit registers.

3. Headless cage fights

The old wall: ten classes means 45 matchups. Testing combo strength and cooldowns by hand would not finish a single round in ten days.

The zero-flesh move:

  • Remove the body. Switch off 3D rendering, switch off audio, drop the 60 fps clock. Keep only the math skeleton: distances, hitboxes, health pools, cooldown timers, startup and recovery frames, state machines.
  • A thousand deathmatches. Drop two AIs driven by behavior trees or scripts into the headless arena and let the CPU run thousands of fully automatic fights in a few minutes.
  • Collapse the win rates. Export a class-versus-class win-rate matrix. Any matchup outside 40%–60% goes back to the model, which trims damage multipliers, stretches cooldowns or adds startup lag to whatever is overtuned. Repeat until the numbers settle into equilibrium and the outcome belongs to the players again: their execution and their mind games.

Open cuts worth stealing

  • Headless arenas elsewhere. The render-off, fight-it-out-a-thousand-times balancing loop isn’t just for fighting games. Point it at any economy or high-mobility mechanic that players will try to break.

核心实现：用「数学公式」消灭「资产管线」

1. 三角函数篡位美术（程序化捏骨与关节驱动）

传统死穴：大模型擅长吐逻辑文本，却没法直接手搓几万个顶点的 Blender 拓扑文件和骨骼权重蒙皮。

零肉身解法：

  • 几何原语拼装躯干：直接在 WebGL / Three.js 空间里，拿基础几何体（Box、Sphere、Cylinder、Capsule）当骨骼节点，拼出人形、铠甲和武器。
  • 数学公式强掰关节：扔掉死板的外部动画文件。每一帧渲染循环（requestAnimationFrame）里，直接用三角函数（Math.sin / Math.cos）、弹簧阻尼模型和四元数插值（Quaternion Slerp）实时算出肩、肘、腰、膝的旋转弧度。前摇是角度蓄力，挥砍是角速度爆发，受击硬直是坐标抖动。
  • 着色器直绘法术：刀光、火球、治疗法阵不贴外部序列帧图片，全由自定义 Shader（片元着色器）和代码粒子发射器当场改写像素色值。

2. 振荡器伪造声带（纯代码声波合成）

传统死穴：去素材网翻找、裁剪、对齐几百个挥刀声和爆炸声，纯代码工作流当场断档。

零肉身解法：

  • 直接调浏览器底层的 Web Audio API，把声音还原成空气振动的数学本质。
  • 用代码拉起振荡器（OscillatorNode：正弦波、锯齿波、方波）和白噪音缓冲（AudioBuffer），串上滤波器（BiquadFilterNode）和音量包络（ADSR GainNode）。
  • 声学映射公式：破空挥砍 = 高通滤波白噪音 + 极速指数衰减；钝器重击 = 低频正弦波从 150 Hz 瞬间滑落到 30 Hz；法术吟唱 = 双振荡器频率调制（FM）叠加混响。所有声音都在攻击判定生效的同一毫秒，由浏览器声卡凭空捏造。

3. 黑盒电子斗蛊（无头模式演化与数值坍缩）

传统死穴：10 个职业意味着 45 组对位与克制关系。靠人工对打测连招强度和冷却时间，十天连一轮都测不完。

零肉身解法：

  • 剥离肉身（渲染与逻辑解耦）：砍掉 3D 画面渲染，砍掉音频输出，解除每秒 60 帧的物理时间枷锁，只留最纯粹的数学骨架：坐标距离、碰撞包围盒、血量池、冷却计时器、技能前后摇帧数和状态机。
  • 千场死斗试炼：在后台（Headless Mode）放进两个由行为树 / 策略脚本驱动的 AI，用 CPU 极限算力在几分钟内跑完数千场全自动死斗。
  • 胜率坍缩铁律：自动导出全职业克制胜率矩阵，把胜率偏离 40%～60% 区间的畸形对局喂回给大模型，自动削减超模技能的伤害倍率、延长冷却或增加前摇破绽，直到所有职业在纯数值博弈上达到动态均衡，把胜负手彻底交还给人类玩家的微操和心理博弈。
